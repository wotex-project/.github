# Security policy

Repositories published under this organization are in scope. Forks and modified
copies are not.

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Use GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting on the affected repository, or email
[hello@wotex.io](mailto:hello@wotex.io) with the affected repository, file, or URL,
steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect. Strip secrets from logs and
screenshots.

Reports are read and answered as time allows, with no response-time promise and
no bounty. Please allow a fix to be released before publishing. Do not access
data that is not yours or test denial of service.
